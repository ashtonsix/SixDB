# Current SeriesPack hardware baseline

The frozen current implementation passes the complete seven-profile
reconciliation. This replaces the older coherent checkpoint for current
performance judgments. Bulk and point access are often competitive with
available same-wire predecessors; ordinary short ranges, ARM narrow geometry
and some H16 construction remain material limitations. Composition benefits
are assessed separately and do not cancel those losses.

predecessor in the same profile/program. Ratios above 1 mean SeriesPack is slower.

The [whole available same-wire table](../evidence/delivery-baseline-20260911/same-wire-comparisons.csv)
preserves both wide56 predecessor variants and every profile. Current Local6
Zen decode is substantially closer than the old 2.438× observation; current
Local4 pure-AVX2 encode is 1.071× rather than the old 1.202×. The retained NEON
Local2 correction reaches same-wire parity. These current results replace
old ratios; they do not retroactively change the earlier measurements.

For u64 full decode, the fastest measured current native family is competitive
with the fastest measured Calico geometry across the 206 descriptions on Zen
(worst ratio 0.946) and GNR(worst 1.016). This is a measured selection over
explicit families/geometries, not runtime automatic dispatch or a same-wire
claim. On V2, narrow Local/u64 decode still loses up to 1.310×; Local/u64 encode
up to 3.658×. Narrow Local/u8 encode can be 12–15.6× slower than striped Calico.
That exposes a physical geometry choice as well as implementation cost:
`preset::bulk_arm` already chooses stripes for its narrow supported widths .
It does not erase the smaller but real same-wire striped kernel gaps above.

Zen H16/u64 encode remains costly against Calico's different plane representations:
Local17/H16 .129318/.067902 ns/value=1.904× and Local23/H16
.132646/.068502=1.936×. Striped17/H16 and 23/H16 use their faster AVX2 family
at 2.210× and 2.228×; the AVX512 family is slower. Local56/H16 AVX512 is
.152112/.072522=2.097× against its headless same-width predecessor, a different
representation. GNR's H16 pattern differs: Local17/H16 is faster than its
Calico control, Local23/H16 is 1.127×, while Local56/H16 remains about 2.08×
against the headless wide predecessor. No common H16 constant explains these.

The [joint H16 projection](../../../seriespack-head-projection/findings.md) won all 122 dense large cases
but lost on small/gapped placements and added 33715 native text bytes. It remains
uninstalled. Current independent placement cases also vary nonmonotonically;
gaps sometimes run faster, which does not make gaps a physical benefit.

## Arbitrary ranges, mutation and composition

Current boundary/runtime fixtures exercise misalignment, stripe crossing,
complete-plus-tail, short and last-lane shapes with independent placements.
For example V2 Local7/u64 origin 1/count 16 takes 15.78 ns; Striped12 aligned 16
7.187 ns versus shifted origin 17/count 16 12.396 ns. Gapped Striped28/H16 aligned 16
12.25 ns versus shifted 16 17.174 ns. These are actual whole-call costs, not a
subtraction-derived generic dispatch tax. Legal output alignments remain legal.

The [ordinary expression integration](../../../seriespack-range-execution/integration.md) and
[clipped striped loop](../../../seriespack-range-execution/striped-fragments.md) were rejected after their
remaining short/suffix losses and code growth. The completed [GNR ordinary-store probe](../../../seriespack-range-execution/ordinary-stores.md) confirms
a small existing seam with substantial get16 wins and remaining short-call
regressions; it does not reinstall those general drivers.

Selected write currently uses scalar per-value read/modify/write. Representative
V2 casing selects 25% and repeatedly overwrites resident storage: Local12/N8193
is 12.155 us for selected write versus 3.550 us for checked whole encode, or
13.598/3.560 us with effects. Those operations have different contracts, and no
same-wire predecessor selected-write control is supplied. Correctness and
issued-write coverage are established; an all-width mutation throughput claim
or a batched native selected writer is not established.

Native authored comparison/reduction, scratch materialization and BEC are
separate whole-consumer evidence. See the [delivery assessment](../../seriespack-assessment.md)
for that composition judgment and candidate decisions.

## Larger source: completed Zen pilot

The current Zen pilot executes 33554432 queries per sample,24 cases and 72 samples,
with independent prefix/checksum/final-state audits after timing. Logical useful
64-byte source-line coverage is 928398016–1054832384 bytes per sample, versus the
host's reported 8 MiB LLC. This accounts useful source addresses; it does not
measure hardware misses. Calico's distinct physical map makes its line counter
inapplicable, while its stream/checksum/state audits still pass.

AVX2 is similar here. Point/dependent access is competitive, but the get16 gap
persists with large useful source coverage; it cannot be explained away as

[retained samples](../evidence/delivery-baseline-20260911/large-source/cases.csv)
