## Known counterexamples

NEON tight extent M65535/B65535, 64-block footprint, no U: Sizes typed reverse+value C8 is 14.91 ns frozen vs 18.46 ns current. Relative Ends is not a universal inverse-throughput winner on NEON. The complete cells are retained.

Initial cursor construction regressions are preserved in `cursor-diagnostic.csv`; final writes above come from a separate source-qualified replay after removing self-referential effect state. No old/new timing samples were pooled.
