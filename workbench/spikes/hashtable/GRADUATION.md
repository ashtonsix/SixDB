# Graduation assessment

**The architecture is worth carrying forward; the spike is not a finished claim of
broad superiority.** Keep the bucket hierarchy, headless packing composition,
compact-list regime, explicit N8 operations, Orbital ownership and cold Loom paths.
Do not graduate the experiment matrix or select universal vectors/slot counts.

The latest instruction is to finish in-flight work and consolidate research.
Those results should inform a deliberate implementation decision before another
campaign. This consolidation does not promote the hashtable prototype. Its small
TuplePack author dependency is included to keep the research build self-contained.

## What matters most before production selection

1. **Choose external multimap ownership with its inline budget and growth.** Use
   full histories and retained-version costs. A universal chain-to-vector swap
   is not supported; the current wide losses are too large to dismiss as a few
   latency nanoseconds.
2. **Resolve the compact lookup and complete-owner costs without discarding tiny
   tables.** Preserve compact storage, and judge an improvement through real
   update/lookup/lifetime consumers.
3. **Close the mutation/lifetime contract.** Decide the required preservation
   granularity with Orbital, then exercise retained readers across growth, merge,
   erase and retirement. Keep allocation authority explicit.
4. **Qualify one coherent configuration.** Reconcile chosen geometry, growth,
   inline/storage owner and hot/cold execution in the same build. Measure serial,
   reciprocal and physical-memory consequences with strong paired baselines.
   Do not multiply every experimental flag into a supported option.

These are finite unresolved choices, not an instruction to explore every possible
variant. The existing architecture, evidence and rejected controls are sufficient
to resume implementation work deliberately once this consolidation is reviewed.
