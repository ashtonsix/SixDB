## Purpose and current emphasis

Find representations and programs which materially reduce useful work for
Engine, and preserve their advantage through writes and changing workloads.
The [original broad brief and performance bar](README.md#how-this-spike-works)
remain. Integer SUM/COUNT, points and replacement histories dominate the evidence
so far; they are not a scope cut to a vertical slice.

Ashton's cold/selective steer prioritises work elimination, useful data movement
and avoiding unnecessary materialisation over winning every hot loop. The
[controlled scan findings](scan/FINDINGS.md) establish a large clustered COUNT
win and a contiguous cold selective win, alongside strong full-SUM and scattered-
cold counterexamples. They condition inputs on one core, not a saturated bus.

Logical planning composition need not imply separate dispatch or materialised
arrays between stages. The [stocktake's seam map](INVENTORY.md#which-things-should-stay-together)
distinguishes execution fusion from semantic/coherence dependencies and physical
placement. The next witnesses must show when exposing a relation changes a
program, against strong opaque complete operations as well as native controls.

## Requirements carried across the fronts

- **Coarse selection:** one bit per block is the working shape; recognise an
  all-excluded group from its block bits. Skip lawful descriptor, payload and
  downstream work before access, retaining an unmasked full-selection path.
  Fine row masks, presence, NULL validity and input dependency grants stay
  distinct. All/most operation families remain owed; no fixed grain or public
  mask interface has been chosen.
- **Separated leading planes:** make one/two-byte planes available through
  Frame where they have useful entropy and independently interpretable
  predicate meaning. Logical heads, residual heads and dictionary-code heads
  have different laws. Neither always splitting last nor universal heads is
  supported by the evidence.
- **Real consumers and alternatives:** preserve native narrow/FOR forms,
  actual system controls, exact arithmetic and complete payload consumption.
  Expose large losses and preparation/maintenance costs, not just relative
  improvements over an older Frame path.
- **Ownership:** distinguish logical coordinates, dictionary/decoding editions
  and physical identity. Use actual Orbital owners and Ikea primitives; Frame
  does not invent a second allocator, MVCC system or geometry implementation.
- **Breadth:** SeriesPack and TuplePack operation families remain in scope.
  String bodies stay with StreamPack and joins with Engine. The grouped hash
  witness is an instrument alongside that broad work, not the selected product.
