# SeriesPack coherent checkpoint, 10 September 2026

This fixed-source checkpoint predates the integrated
[scalar callback change](../../../spikes/ikea-composition/call-boundaries/decoder.md) and subsequent
[runtime-range measurements](../../../spikes/seriespack-range-execution/runtime.md). Its ratios describe
that captured program, not the current working tree. The
[materializer investigation](../../../spikes/seriespack-range-execution/expressions.md) follows the
remaining authored-range costs; its candidates are still separate from the
production driver. Later measurements do not retroactively change this
checkpoint or establish a new complete hardware sweep.

The captured source passes the complete aggregate check on Neoverse V2,
Zen 5 and Granite Rapids. This establishes correctness coverage across all
206 legal descriptions, k=1..64, with the checked, trusted, physical and native
composition paths exercised by the aggregate. It does not establish competitive
performance across the surface. Several individual losses remain material.

The [capture receipt](../evidence/coherent-20260910/capture.json) identifies the
source digest, seven target profiles, commands, binary hashes and three
recoverable worker bundles. [All 16,744 measured cases](../../../spikes/ikea-composition/archive/validation-20260911.md)
retain their three repetitions, item-normalized times and allocation counters;
[summary provenance](../evidence/coherent-20260910/provenance.json) records exact
input JSON hashes and contexts. Bulk cases cover every legal description,
with u64 and the smallest sufficient carrier. Access cases cover all 64
unheaded local widths and all 12 striped widths, including both point strategies.

These are CPU-0, sequential 30 ms minimum repetitions. Bulk uses 8,192 values;
access requests 4 KiB of encoded storage and carries 64 KiB of query indices.
Neither is a cold-access claim. X86 profiles separately permit AVX2 only,
AVX-512BW without VBMI/GFNI, and the full selected feature set. An AVX2-labelled
endpoint in the full profile is allowed those extra features. The comparison
scripts preserve this distinction and do not rank different execution families
as ISA-matched controls. LocalPack/ScanPack remain the primary controls where
available; Calico supplies additional full-width representation controls.

## Point and range access

The regular striped point correction recovers the predecessor's direct byte
load and index-derived shift. For widths 1/2/4, SeriesPack/predecessor time
ratios are 1.003/0.998/0.998 on V2, 0.863/0.895/0.851 on Zen, and
1.015/0.823/1.066 on GNR. The x86 entries use the full-profile AVX-512 family.
This is narrower evidence than claiming every point path is optimal. For
example, random constant-offset table selection remains expensive on Zen.

The ordinary bound range reader still performs too much work for a short
request. Each following query materializes 16 u64 values at an original index
divisible by 16; the columns report time divided by the same-wire predecessor.

| Layout | Zen full AVX-512 | GNR full AVX-512 | V2 NEON |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Local1 | 7.891× | 2.144× | 1.217× |
| Local5 | 6.295× | 2.058× | 0.872× |
| Local7 | 6.009× | 2.057× | 0.891× |
| Local56 | 5.148× | 2.142× | 1.207× |

For scale, Local1 takes 11.244 ns/query on Zen and 13.304 on GNR. The
predecessors take 1.425 and 6.206 ns respectively. Both sides' times matter;
the smaller GNR ratio is not evidence of a faster SeriesPack reader there.
V2's largest remaining narrow striped range ratios are 1.575× at width 3,
1.453× at width 7 and 1.412× at width 5.

The [admitted-region diagnostic](../../../spikes/seriespack-range-execution/prototype/README.md)
separates the physical codec, runtime placement and dynamic range driver.
It reuses the composition source/read/native-executor seams, with count,
alignment and sink type admitted outside the query, while keeping original
coordinates dynamic. The dense control makes an additional explicit placement
admission. Its local instruction and guard evidence is ready; hardware timings
must determine whether this removes the observed costs. It introduces no public
get16 endpoint and does not excuse the current ordinary-range losses.

## Bulk gaps and candidate status

The complete checkpoint includes earlier measured Local3..7 NEON coalescing
and byte-body recovery. Remaining immediate-predecessor encode ratios include
V2 Scan4 1.302×, Scan6 1.255×, Scan2 1.209× and Local2 1.183×. The
[Scan grain experiment](../../../spikes/packed-integer-kernels/neon-scan/v2-decision.md) supports a
Scan4-only follow-up; its Scan6 results do not support spreading that grouping
policy. The Local2 candidate recovers an omitted use of the existing 64-value
coalescing helper. Neither candidate has been included in this checkpoint.

AVX2-only Local4/u8 encode remains 1.202× on Zen and 1.185× on GNR. The
[projected-transpose diagnostic](../../../spikes/packed-integer-kernels/local4/README.md)
tests the predecessor's cheaper final transpose when only four output planes
survive. Raw-array and actual public-endpoint measurements must precede selection.

Zen full-profile Local6/u8 decode regresses to 2.438× its predecessor, while
GNR does not show the same loss. The leaf permutation and affine constants are
unchanged. The new calling context causes Clang to unroll eight 64-value regions
on Zen, versus one in the earlier SeriesPack executable and four on GNR.
That is a concrete hypothesis for a controlled comparison, not yet a demonstrated
cause. Zen's headed narrow encodes also retain large Calico gaps, including
K17/H16 and K23/H16; joint head extraction and compact stores remain separate
experiments. Calico already uses two byte head planes in those shapes.

## Cold workload correction

A subsequent 1 GiB pilot exposed a flaw in the older dependent-index workload:
its fixed index-to-index map enters cycles of only 99 or 559 positions at
width 56. Historical dependent timings therefore do not demonstrate cold reads.
Independent point/get16 indices use a separate stream and are unaffected.

The repaired dependent walk advances a persistent nonce, multiplied by an odd
64-bit Weyl increment before mixing with the previous index and decoded value.
An independent check covers 4,194,304 queries at every width 1..64, including
bounds, preserved dependency/state and occupied positions/required cache lines.
The first unscaled-nonce proposal failed this check and was rejected. The
repaired driver was subsequently measured in the
[V2/GNR 1 GiB pilot](../../../spikes/seriespack-range-execution/admission.md#one-gib-pilot). That later result does
not update this fixed-source checkpoint or establish per-query cache misses.
The [delivery reconciliation](../../../spikes/ikea-composition/seriespack-assessment.md) owns the current
coverage assessment, including remaining larger-source work.

The earlier [access](access-initial.md) and [bulk](bulk-initial.md) notes retain
their original checkpoint evidence. [Composition findings](../../../spikes/ikea-composition/native-regions/findings.md)
own the fused-consumer, physical-grain and carrier experiments.
