## Reasoning worth recovering

The [descriptor guide](../../../spikes/memory-descriptors/README.md#prototype-and-source)
distinguishes the independent implementations and their guarantees. The tuned
finite allocator, retained-backing probe, virtual-growth comparison and object
substrate are not integrated layers. No production allocator or object runtime
was completed.

Two small native histories expose the Object/Allocation boundary in
[composition_probe.cpp](https://github.com/ashtonsix/SixDB/blob/35e63b8416d377f216575d6f8d97668b9bce9ab3/workbench/spikes/orbital-objects/composition_probe.cpp):

- `padding`: a 47-byte body admitted for a 64-byte read requires stable padding.
  Capacity and initialized length alone cannot describe the outstanding use.
- `readers`: a delayed sender outlives publication and context replacement; it
  must send its captured bytes and release the captured backing. The native
  test uses ordinary copying socket I/O and supplied historical snapshots.

Those obligations can be maintained outside a simple allocation service.
[Composition findings](../../../spikes/orbital-mvcc/history/objects.md) also
record why local undo is a synchronous-abort policy, and why a narrow backend
borrow currently keeps a broader stability window alive. Whole-image metadata
and undo work under locks remain prototype costs.

For application declarations, the retained
[read counterexamples](../../transactions/reads.md) show why
absence and changes outside returned rows can invalidate a result. The
[root-publication examples](../../transactions/writes.md#root-publication-two-tiny-counterexamples-and-a-scope-limit)
show why swapping a stale root can lose an independent update. Sound declarations
are a premise of the protocol checks; a general analyzer producing them has not
been verified.

For one path across the research, trace `scenario()` in
[hosted_recovery.cpp](../../../simulator/experiments/hosted_recovery.cpp),
then the [native model's boundaries](../../../simulator/models/README.md)
and [CheckedViews.tla](../../../../orbital/spec/CheckedViews.tla), as explained in
[RUNTIME](../../../../orbital/spec/RUNTIME.md). They study related old-cut computations with
ongoing writers, but are not a proved refinement of one implementation.

## Working lessons

Both roles identified premature elaboration as a failure: plausible fragments,
ownership registries and detailed documents acquired authority before their
composition was understood. The strongest work followed an actual caller,
used independent expected bytes and challenged forbidden intermediate states.
Ashton's request was for conceptual clarity and steerability; shortening prose
alone did not supply either. Tests preserve useful evidence, not an obligation
to preserve the mechanism they tested.
