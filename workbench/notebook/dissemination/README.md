# Dissemination research survey

September 26, 2026: 223 source-linked entries across writes, reads, extensions,
representation, placement, transport, economics, observation and recovery.
This is the durable survey from the retired dissemination spike. Its five detailed
source guides retain the full breadth and original identifiers; [Orbital's mining
guide](../../../orbital/MINING.md) remains the shorter starting point.

The survey was expanded after missing an important old idea: edge-origin broadcast
can avoid the first expensive cloud-egress leg. Preserve that breadth when returning
to networking. Each entry distinguishes the general question, potential benefit,
cost or counterexample, and what was actually explored. Catalogued, explored and
demonstrated remain different claims. This is research context, not selected design.

## Reading the historical evidence

The source inventories and [manifest](survey-source-manifest.json) identify the
September 26 reading boundary. They do not describe a continuously refreshed
survey. Links into retired studies point to their exact Git checkpoint; links to
surviving owners remain local. Recorded source hashes describe the original reading,
not the relocated notebook files.

The [archived findings](https://github.com/ashtonsix/SixDB/blob/275698524c58dea08f19961dbc2940cae68b405b/workbench/spikes/orbital-dissemination/FINDINGS.md)
and adjacent specialized studies retain the routing, sender-choice, reply-incast,
representation, transport, adaptive-credit and delivery comparisons, with selected
results and source recovery. The old event/resource framework is retired. The
[native simulator](../../simulator/README.md) does not yet implement all those
mechanisms, and their synthetic results are not hardware measurements.

The [retirement record](../retired-spikes.md) recovers the complete original study.
Current protocol direction belongs to Orbital; CFT and local-handoff measurements
remain in their independent studies.
